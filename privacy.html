Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Claudio is a personal project used by its owner to view their own health and fitness data. This policy explains what data it uses and how.

Data accessed

With the user's permission through Google sign-in, Claudio reads Google Health API data: activity (steps, distance, calories, active minutes, exercises), health metrics (heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, breathing rate, skin temperature, weight), sleep, and basic profile and settings.

How it is used

The data is used only to show the user their own statistics and to calculate personal recovery and strain scores. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Storage

Google sign-in tokens are stored privately on the owner's Cloudflare account so the app can fetch the data. Health data is fetched on request and cached briefly (up to one hour). Food, water and workout logs the user enters are saved in their own private storage.

Sharing

Data is shown only to the signed-in user. When the user asks the in-app assistant a question, the relevant data is sent to Anthropic's Claude to generate the answer.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
